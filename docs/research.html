Research lab

Each idea is a written rule tested on the same 10 years, after costs. It must be profitable, clear a t-statistic hurdle that rises with every idea ever tried (7 so far), be positive in both halves and in two-thirds of the years, survive double costs, beat random picks on the same days, and survive ±20% changes to its numbers.
